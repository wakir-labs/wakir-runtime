# Governance

Wakir Labs operates as an AI-first organization with human governance:
code is produced by typed AI agent personas under continuous direction,
review, and approval by the supervisory board (human governance per
Brand Statement 4).

Architecture decisions, design reviews, and final release approvals
are human acts documented as Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps. The relevant ADR record
forms part of the work attribution for each release.

The authoritative ADR archive lives in the `decisions/` directory of
the Wakir Labs governance tree. Each ADR carries an `id`, a
`status`, a `decision_date`, and an OpenTimestamps anchor pointer.

For questions about governance scope or decision provenance, contact
Callandor GmbH at the address in `BRAND.md`.
